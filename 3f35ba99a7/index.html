Installed on up to three of your machines, with every update for life. No subscription.

The course

€1,000+→you save it

Ask in one sentence. “Build me a breakout system for EURUSD on H1” — and the Autopilot searches, measures, judges, saves and builds the portfolio, showing you every decision it took for you before it starts.

A tour walks you through each screen in the order of the work. The Help lives inside the program. And Dr. Quant — with your own AI key, or a model running on your machine — knows the judge and the Help, and tells you what each answer cost.

The accounts you burn

$2,000–3,000→the judge first

Let the bullets hit the backtest, not your account.

The Reality Check asks not “did it make money?” but “is the result real?”. It judges every strategy with 13 checks, nine of them on the last stretch of history — the stretch the search never saw. And it knows how many variants were tried to reach each one: the best of a thousand has to prove more than the first.

A real run, made for this page. One sentence to the Autopilot; 9,660 rule sets tried; five strategies kept, the best winning 84% of its trades in the backtest. None came out solid: four passed as fragile, and on the stretch of history it had kept back, only two of those held up. That is its job: a line of the bill you never pay.

It also tests the procedure that found the strategy, repeating the whole search on five windows of history. Then the Quarantine compares what was promised with what happens, before any money is at stake, and Live reads your robots in MetaTrader 5 and JForex, shows how far they drift from their backtest and warns you if one stops or hits its account cut-off.

Training

$1,000–2,000→maybe less

It may not save you the training — or it may. What’s beyond doubt is that you learn with it: it is a laboratory and a blackboard. In the laboratory you write your own rules in the manual builder, test them on years of candles without risking a penny, and see in its charts what they would have done. On the blackboard, Dr. Quant, the AI inside, explains any trading concept you ask it about. And every time a strategy is worthless, it tells you why, with the number in front of you.

It should be every trader’s go-to tool.

The computer

$1,500–3,000→the one you have

It doesn’t need a particle accelerator to start: it runs on the computer you already have, with Linux, Windows 11 or macOS. If yours is more powerful, the genetic search makes use of it — it spreads the work across the cores and tries more rule sets in the same time — but it doesn’t need it. And the licence covers up to three of your computers.

The server

$20–50 a month→once it earns it

You save it until the robot earns it. The Quarantine runs it on your computer against the candles as they arrive and keeps comparing what it does with what its backtest promised. You pay for the server once you know it deserves it.

It doesn’t replace a demo account: it improves on it. A demo belongs to the broker, who closes it after a few weeks so you move to real money, and running the robot on it takes a server or an old computer left on day and night. The Quarantine uses the candles of your real account without sending a single order or risking a cent, and tells you what would have happened. You don’t have to leave anything on: when you open it, it catches up.

And on that day, BotFactoryFX writes it in native code for MQL4, MQL5, Java (JForex), Pine Script and Python (Hyperliquid), compiled for real with each platform’s own compiler, and with protection inside: account cut-off, trading hours and Friday close.
