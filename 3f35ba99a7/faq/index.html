Questions before buying

The short answers to what people ask before paying. If yours isn’t here, write to support@botfactoryfx.com: a person answers.

What it is

What does BotFactoryFX do?

It builds trading strategies by the thousand with a genetic search, then spends most of its effort trying to prove each one is worth nothing: 13 checks, nine of them on history the search never saw. What survives can be watched in Quarantine before any money is at stake, and exported as native code for MetaTrader 4 and 5, JForex, TradingView (Pine Script) and Python. How it works

Will it make me money?

Nobody can promise that, and we don’t. What it does is tell you when a strategy is worthless, with the number in front of you, so you find out on the backtest and not on your account. It gives no signals and no investment advice, and it doesn’t trade for you.

I’m starting out. Is it for me?

Yes, if you want to learn by testing. The manual builder lets you write your own rules and test them on years of candles without risking a penny; a tour walks you through every screen; and Dr. Quant — with your own AI key, or a model running on your computer — explains any trading concept. It won’t teach you to trade on its own, but every time a strategy fails it shows you why.

BotFactoryFX, BFQuarantine or BFInspector?

BFInspector (free) inspects the robot someone wants to sell you and helps whoever trades by hand. BFQuarantine ($199 once) watches a robot you already bought, on the account where it runs, against the backtest it was sold with. BotFactoryFX ($999 once) does everything: builds and judges your own strategies, plus quarantine, portfolio, your accounts on one screen, Dr. Quant and export to six platforms. The three programs

Platforms and data

Which systems does it run on?

Linux, Windows and macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel). On Windows it needs version 10 or 11: it does not start on 7 or 8. It runs on the computer you already have; a more powerful one just lets the search try more rule sets in the same time.

Windows or macOS warns me when I open it. Is that normal?

Yes. The programs aren’t signed yet — that needs Microsoft and Apple certificates we don’t have yet — so the system warns you once. The Read me first in each download says how to get past it, and the sha256 of every file is on the Downloads page.

Does it work with my broker?

It reads history from your broker’s CSV, from your MetaTrader 5 or JForex terminal, from Dukascopy (1,615 instruments), Binance and web3 wallets. The strategies it exports run wherever MetaTrader 4 or 5, JForex, TradingView or Python run.

Does it trade for me?

No. It builds, judges and watches. Its connectors only read your platform and never place an order; the robots it exports trade only if you put them to work on your own platform, under your control.

Licence and updates

How many computers can I use it on?

Up to three. Each gets its own licence key, tied to that computer. Asking again from a computer that already has one doesn’t use up any of the three.

And if I change computer?

Write to support@botfactoryfx.com with your order number and the fingerprint of the new one (the program shows it when it asks for a licence): we free the slot of the computer you no longer use.

Is there a subscription?

No. You pay once and get every update for life. The program tells you when there’s a new version, and you download it from your purchase page; your licence keeps working.

Does the program send my data anywhere?

No. Your strategies, histories and portfolios stay on your computer, and there’s no account. The program only goes online to download the data you ask for, to check for updates, and to talk to the AI you connect, with your own key.
