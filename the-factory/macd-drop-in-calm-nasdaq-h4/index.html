Brickfielder

As the program names it: MACD drop in calm · Nasdaq H4

Nasdaq 100 · 4-hour candles · fixed stops · replayed by StrategyQuant X every Saturday on Dukascopy prices. Found by StrategyQuant X in the 24/7 factory on 13 November 2026; in quarantine since 16 November 2026.

Why “Brickfielder”. Volatility, so a wind: it waits for the market to go calm. The Brickfielder is a hot, dry wind of southern Australia that rises in the country’s deserts in late spring and summer.

Simulated, no real money. Its backtest and its quarantine run on a simulated account; no order is sent to any broker. Its rules, settings and code are not published: this record shows what it did, not how it decides.

It is the third Nasdaq strategy in quarantine, with Ulysses, from BotFactoryFX, and Open below the Bollinger band, from StrategyQuant X, and the first on 4-hour candles. Unlike those two, it sells more often than it buys, so a fall in US stocks would not hit all three alike.

StrategyQuant X built it and BotFactoryFX’s judge scored it, with the same bar as our own finds: Solid, 75 or more, on its own market and again on a similar one, judged from the same date. The judge’s 13 checks were run on StrategyQuant X’s trades; a few, like the random-entry test and the walk-forward, are close equivalents rather than the same, and two cannot be measured there. Its rules do not translate exactly into BotFactoryFX, so it has a quarantine of its own: StrategyQuant X replays it every Saturday on the new candles.

BotFactoryFX’s judge scored StrategyQuant X’s trades and kept each strategy’s score, not a check-by-check report, so that report is not here.
