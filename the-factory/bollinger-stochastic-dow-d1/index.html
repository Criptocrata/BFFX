The judge’s report, check by check

The search that found it on 14 October 2026 kept its score but not this report, so on 1 November 2026 we judged it again exactly as then: same history, costs and cut-off date. It gave the same score: 81/100, Solid. Holds up, including on other instruments and other timeframes. No check failed outright.

What the report does not cover

  • Everything above was measured on 4,067 bars — 13 years, 2013-09-30 to 2026-10-09. Nothing here was tested outside that window.
  • That window does not include the 2008 collapse, so no check above could tell you what this strategy does in one.
  • Deflated Sharpe: counting the 2,355 rule sets the search tried, there is a 49 % probability that this strategy's Sharpe on the history it was chosen on is more than luck — 0.546 per trade over 39 trades, against 0.559 expected from the best of that many rule sets with no edge. The spread of the tried rule sets' Sharpes is taken as that of strategies with no edge, because the search does not record it yet. It looks only at the stretch where the strategy was chosen: a pattern that was real there and then stopped, or a flaw in the data, scores as high as an edge.
  • This was the best of 2,355 different rule sets the search tried. What a search that size scores on data with no edge in it was measured on hourly currency pairs, not on this instrument and timeframe, so there is no reference figure for it here: the more candidates a search tries, the more likely its best is best by luck.
