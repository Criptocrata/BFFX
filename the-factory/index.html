How it is measured

  • Real prices, simulated money. Each strategy reads the live candles of the market where it would trade (Darwinex’s MetaTrader 5, and Hyperliquid for Bitcoin), and BotFactoryFX’s quarantine runs it on them every night, with that market’s costs. Each one has its own simulated account of 10,000 in the market’s currency and a fixed size; no order is sent anywhere.
  • The ones from StrategyQuant X, every Saturday. Their rules do not translate exactly into BotFactoryFX, so they are not in its quarantine: StrategyQuant X itself replays them every Saturday on the week’s Dukascopy candles, and their trades are counted with Darwinex’s costs and a position the size of the 10,000 account, without leverage, because StrategyQuant X sizes its trades its own way. The verdict is still BotFactoryFX’s.
  • Only what came after. A strategy’s count starts the day it went into quarantine. The prices it was found on never count here.
  • The promise is its own backtest. The table puts what its backtest made over the years it was tested next to what it has done live. The dotted line in the chart is that backtest’s result spread evenly over time, drawn from the day it went in: above it, live is ahead of the promise; below, behind.
  • At today’s price. With a fixed lot, what a trade makes on an index or a fund grows with its price, and a backtest that goes back years made most of its trades when the market was much cheaper: as it came, it promised far less than the market moves today. So the promise is its backtest taken to the price of the day it went in, each trade in proportion to its price, with today’s costs. A trade that closed on a stop or a target in fixed points stays as it was: those are worth the same at any price.
  • The verdict is the program’s own. It is the one BotFactoryFX’s quarantine gives on its screen: it holds the live profit factor against the promised one, and says nothing about the money before 20 closed trades.
  • A name of its own, once it passes. A strategy that passes BotFactoryFX’s judge with 75 or more earns a name, from a theme that suits its family: rivers for trend, mountains for breakouts, myths of return for mean reversion, physicists for momentum, winds for volatility, poets for price action and composers for divergence.   • Pairs. Some strategies run twice, with fixed stops and with stops sized to volatility (ATR), to see which holds up better live. Each pair counts as one candidate.

  • A few trades say nothing. These are daily and 4-hour strategies that trade a few times a month. A good or bad start is noise until each one has a few dozen trades.
