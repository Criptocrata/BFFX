Santa Ana

As the program names it: Volatility-led breakout · TLT H4

TLT, long-term US Treasury bonds ETF · 4-hour candles · fixed stops · replayed by StrategyQuant X every Saturday on Dukascopy prices. Found by StrategyQuant X in the 24/7 factory on 16 November 2026; in quarantine since 23 November 2026.

Why “Santa Ana”. Volatility, so a wind: it waits for volatility to rise before buying a breakout, and for it to fall before selling one. The Santa Ana is the hot, dry wind that blows from the deserts of the American West down to the coast of Southern California in autumn and winter, the season in which this strategy was found.

Simulated, no real money. Its backtest and its quarantine run on a simulated account; no order is sent to any broker. Its rules, settings and code are not published: this record shows what it did, not how it decides.

StrategyQuant X found it on BotFactoryFX’s one-hour candles, without the minutes inside them. Replayed minute by minute it made 6 % more trades and kept 87 % of what it earned in the stretch the search never saw. It is the first bond strategy in quarantine, with Paraná, from StrategyQuant X, on emerging-market bonds: they are in the market at the same time a third of the hours.

StrategyQuant X built it and BotFactoryFX’s judge scored it, with the same bar as our own finds: Solid, 75 or more, on its own market and again on a similar one, judged from the same date. The judge’s 13 checks were run on StrategyQuant X’s trades; a few, like the random-entry test and the walk-forward, are close equivalents rather than the same, and two cannot be measured there. Its rules do not translate exactly into BotFactoryFX, so it has a quarantine of its own: StrategyQuant X replays it every Saturday on the new candles.

BotFactoryFX’s judge scored StrategyQuant X’s trades and kept each strategy’s score, not a check-by-check report, so that report is not here.
