What the report does not cover

  • AUDCAD has no USD leg, so pip value could not be converted exactly — P&L and drawdown figures are indicative.
  • Everything above was measured on 26,723 bars — 16 years 9 months, 2010-01-01 to 2026-09-30. Nothing here was tested outside that window.
  • That window does not include the 2008 collapse, so no check above could tell you what this strategy does in one.
  • Deflated Sharpe: counting the 2,558 rule sets the search tried, there is a 89 % probability that this strategy's Sharpe on the history it was chosen on is more than luck — 0.245 per trade over 422 trades, against 0.171 expected from the best of that many rule sets with no edge. The spread of the tried rule sets' Sharpes is taken as that of strategies with no edge, because the search does not record it yet. It looks only at the stretch where the strategy was chosen: a pattern that was real there and then stopped, or a flaw in the data, scores as high as an edge.
  • This was the best of 2,558 different rule sets the search tried. What a search that size scores on data with no edge in it was measured on hourly currency pairs, not on this instrument and timeframe, so there is no reference figure for it here: the more candidates a search tries, the more likely its best is best by luck.
