IYR, US real estate ETF · daily candles · fixed stops · prices from Darwinex’s MetaTrader 5. Found by BotFactoryFX in the 24/7 factory on 1 November 2026; in quarantine since 23 November 2026.

Why “Crane”. Price action, so a poet: it reads the candle before it like a line of verse. Stephen Crane, the American poet and novelist of The Red Badge of Courage, was born on 1 November 1871, the same day of the year this strategy was found; and a crane is what puts up the buildings of the US real estate it trades.

Simulated, no real money. Its backtest and its quarantine run on a simulated account; no order is sent to any broker. Its rules, settings and code are not published: this record shows what it did, not how it decides.

Its stop is about three and a half times its target and almost never fires: it usually sells a day or two later, when a fast RSI turns, so its real risk is a big gap from one day to the next. It is the first real-estate strategy in quarantine, but one more that buys dips in the US.

The judge is BotFactoryFX’s Reality Check: 13 checks on the strategy, nine of them on history the search never saw. Our bar to put one in quarantine is Solid, 75 or more, on its own market and again on a similar one, judged from the same date.

The search keeps the score of each finalist but not its check-by-check report, so that report is not here.
