Paraná

As the program names it: Buy above the lower Bollinger band · EMB H4

EMB, emerging-market bonds ETF · 4-hour candles · fixed stops · replayed by StrategyQuant X every Saturday on Dukascopy prices. Found by StrategyQuant X in the 24/7 factory on 14 November 2026; in quarantine since 23 November 2026.

Why “Paraná”. Trend, so a river: it stays bought while the market holds up, with a stop that follows it. The Paraná, one of South America’s great rivers, flows through Brazil, Paraguay and Argentina, emerging countries that borrow in dollars, like those whose bonds this fund holds.

Simulated, no real money. Its backtest and its quarantine run on a simulated account; no order is sent to any broker. Its rules, settings and code are not published: this record shows what it did, not how it decides.

StrategyQuant X found it on BotFactoryFX’s one-hour candles, without the minutes inside them; replayed minute by minute it earned slightly more. It is almost always buying, so a fall in emerging-market bonds would hit it: in 2022, when they fell, it lost. And Darwinex’s commission and overnight fees take about a quarter of what it earns.

StrategyQuant X built it and BotFactoryFX’s judge scored it, with the same bar as our own finds: Solid, 75 or more, on its own market and again on a similar one, judged from the same date. The judge’s 13 checks were run on StrategyQuant X’s trades; a few, like the random-entry test and the walk-forward, are close equivalents rather than the same, and two cannot be measured there. Its rules do not translate exactly into BotFactoryFX, so it has a quarantine of its own: StrategyQuant X replays it every Saturday on the new candles.

BotFactoryFX’s judge scored StrategyQuant X’s trades and kept each strategy’s score, not a check-by-check report, so that report is not here.
