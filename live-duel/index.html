Why they are called that:

  • Chandrasekhar. Momentum, so a physicist: Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar, Nobel Prize in Physics. Born on 19 October 1910; the duel’s strategies were found on 17 October.
  • Nestor. Mean reversion and trend, so a myth of return: Nestor came back home from Troy, to Pylos, quickly and unharmed.
  • Alcestis. Mean reversion and trend, so a myth of return: Alcestis comes back from the realm of the dead, brought by Heracles.
  • Oceanus. Mean reversion and trend, so a river of myth: for the Greeks, Oceanus was the river that ran round the world and flowed back into itself.
  • Selene. Mean reversion and trend, so a myth of return: Selene, the moon, wanes and comes back every month.
  • Alcyone. Mean reversion and trend, so a myth of return: for Alcyone, the halcyon days of calm come back every winter.
  • Adonis. Mean reversion and trend, so a myth of return: Adonis comes back from the underworld every year.
  • Alpheus. Mean reversion and trend, so a river of myth: Alpheus flows under the sea and comes up again in Sicily.
  • Arethusa. Mean reversion and trend, so a river of myth: Arethusa, the nymph turned spring in Syracuse, whom Alpheus crosses the sea to come back to.
  • Maeander. Mean reversion and trend, so a river of myth: the Maeander winds so much that it gave meanders their name; it turns back on itself and still runs to the sea.
  • Compton. Momentum, so a physicist: Arthur Compton showed that light carries momentum, and won the Nobel Prize for it in 1927.
  • Aurora. Mean reversion and trend, so a myth of return: Aurora, the dawn, comes back every morning.
  • Liszt. Divergence, so a composer: in counterpoint, two voices move apart. Franz Liszt was born on 22 October 1811; the duel’s strategies were found on 17 October.

How it is measured

  • The same prices. The S&P 500 from Dukascopy, the daily candles that have closed since Monday 19 October 2026. Neither program had seen them.
  • The same contestants as in the duel, 13 a side. The 13 StrategyQuant X approved with its own tests, and the 13 that BotFactoryFX’s judge rated highest among the 24 it scored 45 or more (until 4 November 2026 all 24 played). Both sides chose them with prices only up to September 2022.
  • Each one on its own engine. Every Saturday StrategyQuant X re-tests its strategies itself on the new prices. BotFactoryFX’s engine measures its own, with the stops sized to volatility (ATR), the way the program sends a strategy to quarantine.
  • The same costs. 0.5 points of spread on each trade for both; BotFactoryFX also pays 0.1 of slippage.
  • The bar is chance, not profit. Each trade is repeated 500 times, entering at a random hour since 19 October, with the same direction, the same time in the market and the same cost. A strategy beats random when it did better than more than half of those draws. Making money in a rising market is not enough.

Read it with care

  • Few trades, for weeks. These are daily strategies that trade a few times a month. Until each side has dozens of trades, the score says how it is going, not who wins.
  • Each side’s strategies resemble each other. Several made the same trade on the same day: so far BotFactoryFX’s 31 trades are only 11 different ones, and StrategyQuant X’s 52 are 27. In practice each side is a handful of independent bets.
  • We built BotFactoryFX, and the duel too. StrategyQuant X runs with a trader’s templates, not the best setup anyone could build. We publish it whoever is ahead, and every week stays on this page.
