How it is measured

  • The same prices. The S&P 500 from Dukascopy, the daily candles that have closed since Monday 19 October 2026. Neither program had seen them.
  • The same contestants as in the duel, 13 a side. The 13 StrategyQuant X approved with its own tests, and the 13 that BotFactoryFX’s judge rated highest among the 24 it scored 45 or more (until 4 November 2026 all 24 played). Both sides chose them with prices only up to September 2022.
  • Each one on its own engine. Every Saturday StrategyQuant X re-tests its strategies itself on the new prices. BotFactoryFX’s engine measures its own, with the stops sized to volatility (ATR), the way the program sends a strategy to quarantine.
  • The same costs. 0.5 points of spread on each trade for both; BotFactoryFX also pays 0.1 of slippage.
  • The bar is chance, not profit. Each trade is repeated 500 times, entering at a random hour since 19 October, with the same direction, the same time in the market and the same cost. A strategy beats random when it did better than more than half of those draws. Making money in a rising market is not enough.

Read it with care

  • Few trades, for weeks. These are daily strategies that trade a few times a month. Until each side has dozens of trades, the score says how it is going, not who wins.
  • Each side’s strategies resemble each other. Several made the same trade on the same day: so far BotFactoryFX’s 15 trades are only 5 different ones, and StrategyQuant X’s 18 are 11. In practice each side is a handful of independent bets.
  • We built BotFactoryFX, and the duel too. StrategyQuant X runs with a trader’s templates, not the best setup anyone could build. We publish it whoever is ahead, and every week stays on this page.
